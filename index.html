About the Convention

Soar together as one Mindanao

The 3rd Eagles Mindanao Convention brings together members, officers, and guests from across the island for a weekend of fellowship, leadership, and community service.

Fellowship

Reconnect with EAGLES from Zamboanga to Caraga and build lasting bonds across the island.

Outreach programs that give back to Mindanao communities — the heart of our mission.
